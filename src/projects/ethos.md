ethos is the habit half of the personal cloud, on its own: what you meant to do
and how often, what you actually did, and the cue that should bring it up. It
does not schedule anything — it feeds [auto-cal](/projects/auto-cal/), which
does.

Early-stage. The scope is deliberately one thing.
