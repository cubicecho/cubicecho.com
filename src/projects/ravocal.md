ravocal helps a group land on a decision together: propose the options —
meeting times, places, whatever is up for a vote — collect everyone's ranked
preferences, and let ranked-choice voting settle on the winner the most people
can live with, rather than the one that got a plurality.

Early-stage. Stack: TypeScript, GraphQL, Docker.
