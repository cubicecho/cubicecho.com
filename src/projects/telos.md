Projects hold todos. Todos can depend on other todos, and a todo you are
waiting on is one you cannot tick off yet — telos knows that and says so.
Labels attach to projects and todos alike. That is the whole product: a
sidebar of projects with open and total counts, a title and a checkbox, and
adding one is a sentence and Enter.

Runs as one container plus Postgres.

Stack: TypeScript, GraphQL, Drizzle, Postgres, Docker.
